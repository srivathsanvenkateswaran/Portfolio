Thirteen products in seven weeks. Four of them run someone else's business.

Between 2 July and 19 August 2026 I shipped thirteen products across 1,765 commits. Not prototypes: four of them are open on somebody's screen this morning.

I build the software small businesses actually run on. A gym still writing receipts by hand. A distributor whose money was locked inside an ERP nobody could query. A consultancy with a good record and no way to show it. Most of these started with somebody describing a problem that was quietly costing them money every month.

By day I write backend systems at SILQ, and spent three years before that on Caterpillar's enterprise search platform. Everything below is what I built after hours, with its real status attached: in use, just built, or pitched to somebody who never wrote back.
